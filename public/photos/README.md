# 📸 Best Friend Birthday Photos

Put your photos here!

Simple steps:
1. Drop your images into this folder (`public/photos/`).
2. Rename them to:
   - `photo1.jpg`
   - `photo2.jpg`
   - `photo3.jpg`
   - `photo4.jpg`
   - `photo5.jpg`
   - `photo6.jpg`
   - `photo7.jpg`
   - `photo8.jpg`
   - `hero.jpg` (optional for the main hero card)
   - `final.jpg` (optional for the final scrapbook letter)
3. The website will automatically display them!

Alternatively, you can click the small **"✨ Edit memories"** button on the bottom corner of the website to drag-and-drop your photos directly from your computer without touching any code!
